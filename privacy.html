Zmanim 87

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Zmanim 87 is an Android app that shows Jewish prayer times (zmanim), the Hebrew date and announcements on a tablet or TV box. This policy explains what the app reads, where that data goes and how to remove it.

The short version

  • Everything the app reads or downloads stays on your device.
  • The app has no server of its own. Nothing is sent to the developer.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no data is sold or shared.

Google Drive (read-only)

If you turn on "Google Drive links" on the app's Gmail page, you allow the app read-only access to your Google Drive (drive.readonly) through Google's own permission screen. The app uses it for one thing: when mail from a sender you approved contains a Google Drive link, the app downloads the photo or video behind that link to your device. For each such link it reads the file's name, type, size and contents, and it reads your account's email address once, to show which account you connected. It does not browse, list, change or delete anything in your Drive.

Gmail

If you connect a Gmail account (with an app password you create in your Google account), the app checks your Inbox for mail from senders you approved and saves their photos and videos on the device. Gmail does the filtering, so the app never sees other mail. Checking never changes your mailbox. The app sends mail or changes labels only when you do it yourself on the app's Gmail page. The app password is kept in the device's secure storage.

Other data

  • Location: used on the device to calculate prayer times. To name the place, the app may use Android's built-in geocoder, and to show the weather it sends the location to weatherapi.com.
  • Microphone: used only when you record an announcement. Recordings are saved on the device.
  • Location database: downloaded from GeoNames; no personal data is sent.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Zmanim 87's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

  • Drive access: on the app's Gmail page, "Google Drive links" > Remove, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Gmail: on the app's Gmail page, Account > Remove, and delete the app password in your Google account.
  • Downloaded photos and videos are in the device's Documents/gmailMedia folder and can be deleted at any time. Uninstalling the app removes its private data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.
