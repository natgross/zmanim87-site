Zmanim 87

Jewish prayer times on an always-on tablet or TV box.

Zmanim 87 is an Android app for a dedicated display at home or in a shul. It shows the zmanim for your location, the Hebrew date and the weekly parsha, speaks or plays announcements before the times you choose, and keeps a list of custom events.

Photos and videos from family

The app can show photos and videos that family members email you. You pick the approved senders; the app downloads their pictures and videos from your Gmail to the device. When Gmail sends large attachments as Google Drive links, the app can download those too, with read-only Drive access that you allow on Google's own screen.

Privacy

Everything stays on your device. The app has no server of its own and sends nothing to the developer. See the Privacy Policy.
